Project 02

QuestGo

QuestGo is a platform that connects CIT-U students who need help with tasks to other students who are willing to complete those tasks for a reward. Students can post, browse, and accept “Quests,” then communicate and coordinate meetups through the platform. The system tracks the Quest from Available → Accepted → In Progress → Completed, with unaccepted Quests expiring after 30 minutes.
