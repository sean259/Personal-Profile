Project 02

QuestGo

QuestGo connects CIT-U students who need help with tasks to students willing to complete them for a reward. Students post, browse, and accept “Quests,” then coordinate meetups through the platform. Each Quest runs Available → Accepted → In Progress → Completed, and unaccepted Quests expire after 30 minutes.
